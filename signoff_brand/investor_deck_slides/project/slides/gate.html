The moat is the gate.

Four tiers, in code.

Every request the agent makes is classified before it runs. The tier decides what the owner has to do, and the log keeps every decision, including the refusals.

0

Read

Runs free, logged.

"What's my balance?"
"What do I owe in tax?"

1

Reversible

Runs, logged, can be undone.

"Add a bill from Hostway"
"Close the month"

2

Outward

The owner taps a card.

"Invoice Acme $4,000"
"Reply to the new-bank-details email": refused

3

Money

Card, one-time code, bank approval, new-payee cap, daily limit.

"Pay Priya $1,200"
"Pay Builder Co $3,000": refused

A prompt hidden in an email cannot move money, because the email never reaches the gate. Only the agent's proposal does, and the gate reads code, not prose.
