FOUNDERS WHO WILL RAISE

"I'll raise money"

The owner pays a subscription and a flat fee per raise event.

ONE-PERSON BUSINESSES

"I mow lawns on weekends"

Home-state LLC, EIN, bank account, QuickBooks with the bank feed on, bills paid on their due dates, invoices and payment links, a share of every deposit reserved for tax, quarterly payments and the annual report filed, cards locked to one kind of spending, a CPA before the first return. The owner pays a subscription and fees on money movement.

Formation is the acquisition wedge. The bank account is the retention wedge. A small business that later raises is converted, with the owner's approval, and keeps everything.
