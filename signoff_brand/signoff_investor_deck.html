One-person businesses

Home-state LLC, EIN, bank account, QuickBooks with the bank feed on, invoices that chase themselves, a quarter of profit moved to a tax reserve, cards locked to one kind of spending, a CPA before the first filing. The owner pays a subscription and fees on money movement.
