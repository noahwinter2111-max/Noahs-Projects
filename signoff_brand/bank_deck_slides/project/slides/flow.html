How an account opens.

Dark steps are yours.

SIGNOFF

THE BANK

SIGNOFF

1

Company formed

Owner approves the filing. State accepts. EIN issued.

2

Documents sent

Certificate, EIN letter and operating agreement go to the bank by API, with the owner's approval.

3

Owner verified

ID, selfie, sanctions and watchlists, beneficial-owner questions, on the bank's own screens.

4

Decision sent back

One message: account open, or declined. No reasons travel with a decline.

5

Modules unlock

Payments, cards and QuickBooks stay locked until the bank's "open" message arrives.

Signoff cannot skip the bank's steps, shorten them, or see what happens inside them. The prototype simulates them with a webhook so the sequence can be shown end to end.
