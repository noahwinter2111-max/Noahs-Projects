Four tiers.

Decided by code, not by the model.

0

Read

Balances, inbox, reports. Runs freely. Logged.

1

Reversible

Drafts and internal writes. Runs, logged, can be undone.

2

Outward

A filing, an email, an invoice, a contract. The owner taps approve on a card showing exactly what goes out.

3

Money

The card, plus a six-digit code to the owner's phone, plus the bank's own approval. New payees capped. Daily limits apply.

The gate also refuses on its own. An email that tells the agent to change a payee's bank details is flagged, never acted on, and the refusal is logged next to the approvals.
