Three things the bank does.

All of them already yours.

A sponsor bank does these for every fintech program it runs. Signoff adds the front door and nothing else.

01 · VERIFY THE OWNER

Identity, selfie match, sanctions and watchlists, beneficial-owner questions. Signoff hands over the formation documents and waits. It never sees an ID number or a Social Security number.

02 · HOLD THE ACCOUNT

The company's money sits at the bank, including the tax reserve and the raised sub-account for investor money. Signoff has read access to balances and transactions and nothing else.

03 · EXECUTE PAYMENTS

Signoff sends a payment instruction only after the owner approves a card and types a one-time code. The bank applies its own limits and fraud checks before anything moves.

Signoff never takes custody of funds and never acts as a money transmitter.
