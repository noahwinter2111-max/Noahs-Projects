Who gets in.

And who decides.

SIGNOFF SCREENS FOR

Scope, not people

Businesses the bank will not take are declined before anything is filed: money services, gambling, adult, cannabis, weapons, crypto exchanges.

One owner per account, with a phone that receives the one-time codes. A repeat sanctions screen every 90 days, through the bank's provider, with results going to the bank.

THE BANK DECIDES

People, under its own policy

Whether the person is who they say they are. Whether anything in their history means the bank will not open the account. Whether to close one later.

Signoff keeps no record of why someone was declined and runs no background checks of its own. A decline from the bank means the owner cannot use Signoff with this bank. That is the whole rule.

Any use of criminal-history or consumer-report data sits with the bank and its adverse-action and fair-lending obligations, where it belongs.
