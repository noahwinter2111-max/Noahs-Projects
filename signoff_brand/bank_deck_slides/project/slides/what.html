One chat builds the company.

Then it runs it.

From "I want to start a business" to a formed company with an EIN, a bank account, QuickBooks and email, then the bills, taxes, invoices and payroll that follow. For founders who will raise and for one-person businesses that never will.

01 · THE AGENT

Proposes

It drafts the filing, the payment, the invoice, the reply. It never holds money and never signs for the owner.

02 · THE GATE

Decides

Plain code checks every action: what tier it is, what it needs, whether the stage is unlocked. The model cannot talk past it.

03 · THE OWNER

Signs off

Outward actions end in a card they tap. Money adds a one-time code. The bank runs its own approval on top.

A working prototype of the whole journey is live today. Three minutes takes you from a sentence to a payment that needs a code.
