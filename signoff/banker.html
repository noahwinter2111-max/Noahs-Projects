What Signoff sends your bank, step by step.

Signoff forms a company and runs its books and email from a chat. The bank verifies the owner, holds the money and executes every payment. Built by Evan Winter, founder of Truffles AI, a credit-intelligence platform used by 13 financial institutions. This page walks you through the working prototype and shows exactly where the bank sits.

02 · Who does what

The bank keeps everything a regulator expects a bank to keep.

The bank

Decides and holds

  • Verifies the owner: identity, selfie, sanctions and watchlists, beneficial-owner questions, on its own screens.
  • Decides whether to open the account, under its own written policy, including any criminal-history or consumer-report rule.
  • Holds the money. Executes every payment after its own limits and fraud checks.
  • Decides whether to close an account later.

Signoff

Prepares and asks

  • Forms the company and delivers the documents by API: certificate, EIN letter, operating agreement.
  • Has read access to balances and transactions, nothing more.
  • Sends a payment instruction only after the owner approves a card and types a one-time code.
  • Keeps no record of why anyone was declined, and runs no background checks of its own.

A decline from the bank means the owner cannot use Signoff with that bank. That is the whole rule.

04 · The ask

Thirty minutes, then a sandbox.

First

A conversation

With whoever runs fintech partnerships. We show the prototype and you tell us what would stop you.

Then

Sandbox access

Test accounts and the onboarding, balance and payment-instruction APIs, so the simulated bank becomes a real one with fake money.

Then

Your onboarding rules in writing

Which industries, which documents, which limits. Signoff's gate is configured from them, not the other way round.

Later

A pilot

A small number of accounts from the waitlist, opened through Signoff, reviewed together after 90 days.
