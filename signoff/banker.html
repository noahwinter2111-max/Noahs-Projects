What Signoff sends your bank, step by step.

Signoff forms a company from a chat and then runs it: bills, invoices, taxes, payroll, investor money. The bank verifies the owner, holds the money and executes every payment. Built independently; Evan Winter is the founder of record and the person who signs. This page walks you through the working prototype and shows exactly where the bank sits.

Who does what

The bank keeps everything a regulator expects a bank to keep.

The bank

Decides and holds

  • Verifies the owner: identity, selfie, sanctions and watchlists, beneficial-owner questions, on its own screens.
  • Decides whether to open the account, under its own written policy, including any criminal-history or consumer-report rule.
  • Holds the money. Executes every payment after its own limits and fraud checks.
  • Holds the sub-accounts too: the tax reserve and the raised account for investor money.
  • Decides whether to close an account later.

Signoff

Prepares and asks

  • Forms the company and delivers the documents by API: certificate, EIN letter, operating agreement.
  • Has read access to balances and transactions, nothing more.
  • Sends a payment instruction only after the owner approves a card and types a one-time code.
  • Sends standing instructions, like bills on due dates or a reserve rule, only after one code, and logs every time they fire.
  • Keeps no record of why anyone was declined, and runs no background checks of its own.

A decline from the bank means the owner cannot use Signoff with that bank. That is the whole rule.

The account

What the account does all day, and why the deposits stay.

The owner runs the whole company from Signoff, and Signoff works only with this account. Every one of these is live in the prototype.

Bills on their due dates

Bills arrive from the inbox. One code schedules the batch; each goes out the morning it is due if the balance covers it. Anything over the owner's limit is held.

Bank executes · Signoff instructs
Autopay per payee, with a cap

A rule per payee, approved once. Future bills under the cap pay themselves; over the cap they wait for the owner.

Standing instruction · logged each time
Tax reserve, automatically

A share of every deposit moves to a sub-account the day it lands. Quarterly estimated tax goes to the IRS from that account with a code.

Sub-account at the bank
Getting paid

Payment links, monthly subscriptions and invoices that chase themselves. Money settles into this account and matches itself in QuickBooks. Refunds need a code.

Card processor settles here
Investor money kept apart

When a SAFE is signed the bank shows the expected wire and matches it when it lands. The money sits in a raised sub-account; moving it to operating needs a code.

Second sub-account
Payroll and cards

Owner salary through payroll for a C corp, an owner draw for an LLC. Virtual cards locked to one merchant rule, frozen with a tap. Low-balance alerts hold standing payments.

Bank's card program

Account open, every payment, every sub-account move, every card: the bank sees all of it and executes all of it.

The integration

What the integration looks like.

Drafted against a sandbox-style business banking API so it can be tested with fake money. The same shape works for any bank. Nothing is wired until there are keys and a sandbox.
